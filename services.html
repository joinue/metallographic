On-Site Installation

A PACE technician handles delivery, placement, calibration, and verification so your equipment is producing results from day one, with no guesswork on your end.

  • Equipment setup and calibration
  • Initial testing and verification
  • Utility and space-requirement guidance
  • Documentation and handoff

Training

Hands-on sessions tailored to your team's skill level, from day-one operator basics to advanced metallographic techniques and process optimization.

  • On-site or remote training options
  • Basic operation and maintenance
  • Advanced techniques and optimization
  • Customized training for your applications

Maintenance Visits

Schedule a technician visit on your timeline for preventive upkeep, diagnostics, or repairs. No annual contract required.

  • Flexible scheduling
  • Preventive maintenance
  • Diagnostic and repair services
  • Equipment optimization

Upgrades & Optimization

Outgrowing your current setup? We handle equipment upgrades, retrofits, and process optimization to help your lab keep pace with changing requirements.

  • Equipment upgrades and retrofits
  • Workflow and process optimization
  • Software and firmware updates
  • Capacity and throughput assessments
