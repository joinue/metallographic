Sample Specifications

Soft = Al, Cu, annealed steels. Medium = mild & low-alloy steels. Hard = hardened tool steels. Very Hard = nitrided, case-hardened, ceramics, carbides. Cemented carbides and ceramics are typically rated in HRA or HV — pick "Very Hard" or "Unknown" if HRC doesn't apply.
