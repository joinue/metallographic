Why KeepPACE

Equipment downtime costs more than service.

A cutter that won't hold a profile or a press with low ram pressure stalls analysis, pushes out QC deadlines, and sends work to expensive third-party labs. KeepPACE catches the small issues before they turn into downtime.

Maximize uptime

Scheduled inspections catch wear, drift, and contamination before they take a system offline. Production samples don't sit waiting on a service call.

Protect your investment

Factory-trained technicians and OEM parts let a cutter, press, or polisher run its full design life instead of limping along on borrowed time.

Process consistency

Reviewed prep procedures and annual consumable check-ins help a new operator on second shift get to the same microstructure as your most senior tech.

How It Works

A clear cadence. No chasing service calls.

Every KeepPACE agreement runs on the same rhythm. We handle scheduling, paperwork, and reporting so your team can focus on samples.

1

Onboarding & baseline

We inventory every covered system, document its current configuration, and get on the calendars of your facilities and procurement contacts.

2

Scheduled maintenance

Annual or biannual visits (depending on tier) for inspection, adjustment, and proactive part replacement, booked around your production windows.

3

Priority response

When something does go wrong, a dedicated service contact works the issue from remote diagnostics through on-site repair. You don't rebuild the story each call.

4

Annual review & optimization

Each year we look back at service activity, consumable usage, and process drift, then recommend adjustments for the next.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often during procurement and renewal conversations.

Which equipment is eligible for KeepPACE?

Any PACE Technologies metallographic system: cutters, mounting presses, grinder/polishers, vibratory polishers, and hardness testers. Plans cover the system itself plus its dedicated peripherals (recirculating tanks, cooling units, controllers).

Can we combine multiple systems on one plan?

Yes. Enterprise Basic covers up to 10 systems on one agreement; Standard, Plus, and Premium scale up from there. Most multi-system labs prefer one enterprise agreement over juggling per-system plans.

Do plans cover travel and on-site labor?

Tiered annual service credits offset on-site labor. Travel is billed separately at standard rates and is consolidated onto one invoice for multi-site visits when we can group them.

How are emergencies handled?

Plus and Enterprise plans get priority response with a named escalation contact. We start with remote diagnostics (phone or video) and dispatch a technician on-site if we can't resolve it remotely. Loaner equipment is available on supported plans.

What service documentation do you provide?

Every PM visit and repair gets written up: date, technician, what we found, what we changed, and any parts replaced. Records are available as PDFs or can be pushed into your maintenance system. If you need a specific format for your internal documentation, tell us up front and we'll work to match it.

Is there a minimum term?

Individual plans run a year. Enterprise agreements run 1, 2, or 3 years with pricing locked for the term.

What if we want to add coverage mid-term?

Systems can be added at any time and pro-rated to the current term. A new install drops into your existing agreement; you don't restart paperwork.
