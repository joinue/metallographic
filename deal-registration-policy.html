Deal Registration Policy

Effective May 13, 2026 · Policy Version 1.0

This Deal Registration Policy explains how authorized PACE channel sellers (Distributors, Resellers, and Channel Partners) may register specific end-customer opportunities for protection against parallel pursuit by other PACE channels, including PACE direct sales. It supplements the deal-registration provisions of each Authorized Channel Seller's applicable agreement with PACE.

Reference document. PACE may modify this Policy at any time. The current version is always available at metallographic.com/deal-registration-policy.html. To register an opportunity or ask about an existing registration, email pace@metallographic.com.

01

Purpose

This Deal Registration Policy ("Policy") explains how authorized PACE channel sellers (each, an "Authorized Channel Seller") may register specific end-customer opportunities with PACE for protection against parallel pursuit by other PACE channels, including PACE direct sales. It supplements the deal-registration and reserved-account provisions of each Authorized Channel Seller's applicable agreement with PACE (Distributor Agreement, Reseller Agreement, or Channel Partner Agreement). In the event of a conflict, this Policy controls as to deal-registration mechanics and the applicable agreement controls as to all other matters.

Deal registration exists for one purpose: to give a Channel Seller fair protection for the sales effort they put into a specific opportunity. Registration is not a guarantee of a sale, an exclusivity over a customer, or compensation for opportunities PACE was already pursuing.

02

Scope

Summary

Anyone with an active PACE Channel Agreement can use this policy. The exact mechanics differ slightly by tier.

This Policy applies to:

  • Authorized Distributors operating outside the United States, under the Reserved Accounts and Deal Registration provisions of the Distributor Agreement
  • Authorized Resellers operating in the United States and Canada, on equipment opportunities under the Reseller Agreement
  • Authorized Channel Partners operating in the United States, on equipment opportunities under the Channel Partner Agreement

Authorized Channel Sellers may register opportunities under this Policy from the Effective Date of their Channel Agreement until its termination or expiration. Referral Partners under the PACE Referral Terms do not register opportunities under this Policy; lead submission and exclusivity for Referral Partners is governed by the Referral Terms.

03

What Can Be Registered

Summary

Equipment opportunities only for Resellers and Channel Partners. Distributors may also register material consumables opportunities. No registration for catalog reorders or speculative interest.

An opportunity is registrable when all of the following are true:

  • It involves a specific, identifiable End Customer (legal entity name and location)
  • The Channel Seller has invested or is preparing to invest material sales effort (quote preparation, on-site demonstration, formal proposal, or similar)
  • For Resellers and Channel Partners: the opportunity is for PACE equipment (sectioning saws, mounting presses, grinder-polishers, vibratory polishers, microscopes, hardness testers, or related capital equipment)
  • For Distributors: the opportunity is for PACE equipment, or for a material consumables order defined as a single order or short-term order series with an expected net invoice value of $10,000 USD or more

The following are not registrable under this Policy:

  • Standard catalog consumables orders below the Distributor threshold above
  • Single-SKU re-orders from existing PACE customers
  • Speculative interest without an identified End Customer
  • Opportunities where the End Customer has already requested a direct quote from PACE
  • Opportunities involving a Reserved Account (see Section 8)
04

How to Register

Summary

Email PACE before you start serious sales effort. Provide customer name, location, and what equipment they're scoping.

To register an opportunity, the Channel Seller shall email pace@metallographic.com with the subject line "Deal Registration — [Channel Seller Name] — [End Customer Name]" and include the following information:

  1. Channel Seller information: Legal name, primary contact for this opportunity, and type of Channel Agreement (Distributor, Reseller, or Channel Partner)
  2. End Customer information: Legal entity name, location (city, state or province, country), industry or use case if known, primary technical contact if known
  3. Opportunity scope: PACE equipment models or product categories under consideration; for Distributors registering a consumables opportunity, the products and expected order value; anticipated decision timing; anticipated total net invoice value
  4. Status: Where the Channel Seller is in the sales cycle (initial contact, evaluation, quote, etc.) and any known prior engagement between the End Customer and PACE

PACE may require additional information before approving a registration. A registration is "complete" when PACE has received all the information required under this Section.

05

PACE's Response

Summary

PACE responds within 5 business days with one of: Approved, Reserved, or Co-engagement.

PACE shall respond to a complete registration within five (5) business days with one of the following determinations:

  • Approved. The opportunity is registered to the Channel Seller for the applicable Registration Period (Section 6). Other PACE Channel Sellers may not be approved for the same registered opportunity during this period. PACE direct sales will not proactively engage the registered End Customer on the registered opportunity during the period.
  • Reserved. The End Customer is a Reserved Account (Section 8) or is already the subject of an active PACE direct sales pipeline opportunity. The registration is declined and PACE will handle the opportunity directly. No referral fee, commission, or other compensation is owed to the Channel Seller in connection with the declined opportunity.
  • Co-engagement. PACE elects to participate alongside the Channel Seller on the opportunity, on terms agreed at the time of registration. This may include shared technical resources, joint customer calls, or split sales credit.

PACE's response is binding on PACE for the duration of the Registration Period, subject to the modification and revocation provisions in Section 9.

06

Registration Period and Renewal

Summary

Equipment registrations last 180 days. Distributor consumables registrations last 90 days. Renewable if the deal is still progressing.

An Approved registration is effective for:

  • 180 calendar days from the date of PACE's approval, for equipment opportunities
  • 90 calendar days from the date of PACE's approval, for Distributor consumables opportunities

Renewal. The Channel Seller may request renewal of an Approved registration by emailing PACE at least ten (10) business days before the Registration Period expires, with a status update showing material continued progress on the opportunity (recent customer meetings, outstanding quotes, evaluation milestones, etc.). PACE may grant or decline renewal in its discretion. Renewals are typically granted for an additional sixty (60) days but PACE may grant longer or shorter renewals based on the opportunity's status.

Lapse. If a registered opportunity has not closed by the end of the Registration Period (including any renewals), and the Channel Seller does not request a further renewal, the registration lapses and the End Customer becomes available to other PACE channels and to PACE direct sales.

07

Conflict Resolution

Summary

First-in-time wins. PACE direct sales can override if the customer was already in PACE's pipeline. Disputes go to PACE's commercial team and PACE's decision is final.

The following rules govern conflicts between multiple registration requests for the same End Customer:

  • First-in-time. Where two or more Channel Sellers attempt to register the same opportunity for the same End Customer, the earlier complete registration (measured by the timestamp PACE receives all required information under Section 4) takes priority.
  • PACE direct override. If PACE direct sales is already in active engagement with the End Customer at the time of registration receipt (defined as an outstanding quote, pending order, active opportunity in PACE's CRM, or named pipeline opportunity), PACE may decline the registration as Reserved regardless of timing.
  • Reseller vs. Channel Partner. Where a Reseller and a Channel Partner both seek to register the same opportunity, first-in-time applies. The two tiers are not differentiated for registration priority.
  • Domestic vs. international. Where a domestic Reseller or Channel Partner registers a US or Canadian opportunity, and an international Distributor seeks to register the same opportunity by reason of the End Customer having operations in the Distributor's Territory, PACE will evaluate which channel is the more appropriate primary contact and may grant a Co-engagement or split-credit arrangement. Domestic channel relationships are not automatically subordinate to international Distributors and vice versa.

PACE's decision on conflict resolution is final and not subject to internal appeal beyond PACE's commercial leadership.

08

Reserved Accounts

Summary

PACE keeps certain customers off-limits to channel registration: anyone PACE invoiced in the last 36 months, or anyone PACE is actively selling to right now.

"Reserved Accounts" are End Customers that, at the time of registration: (i) were invoiced by PACE within the prior thirty-six (36) months for any Product or service; or (ii) are the subject of an active sales opportunity in PACE's records, including outstanding quotes, pending purchase orders, or named pipeline opportunities. PACE retains the right to sell Products directly to Reserved Accounts on any terms PACE determines, without obligation to any Channel Seller and without any referral fee, commission, or other compensation.

PACE does not publish its customer list. The registration process under this Policy is the mechanism by which a Channel Seller determines whether a prospective End Customer is a Reserved Account. A Channel Seller's failure to register an opportunity creates no right against PACE if the End Customer later proves to be a Reserved Account.

09

Modifications and Revocation

Summary

PACE can update this Policy. PACE can revoke a registration if the Channel Seller misrepresents the opportunity, abandons it, or violates other parts of the Channel Agreement.

Policy Modifications. PACE may modify this Policy at any time by publishing an updated version at metallographic.com/deal-registration-policy.html. Material changes are effective for new registrations submitted after publication. Existing approved registrations remain governed by the version of the Policy in effect at the time of approval.

Revocation. PACE may revoke an Approved registration upon written notice to the Channel Seller if any of the following occurs:

  • The Channel Seller materially misrepresented the opportunity at the time of registration (for example, the End Customer was already engaged with PACE direct sales and the Channel Seller was aware of this)
  • The Channel Seller abandons the opportunity, defined as no material activity for sixty (60) consecutive days, without requesting a renewal under Section 6
  • The Channel Seller breaches any other material provision of its Channel Agreement, including the MAP Policy, anti-bribery and anti-corruption requirements, export-control requirements, or marketplace restrictions
  • The Channel Seller's Channel Agreement terminates or expires

Upon revocation, the End Customer becomes available to other PACE channels and to PACE direct sales without obligation to the revoked Channel Seller.
